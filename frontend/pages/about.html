About CareTrace

Longitudinal, state-aware Clinical Decision Support — rules from real clinical guidelines, every recommendation reviewed by a clinician.

What it is

{{site_name}} is an Agentic AI Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) built for personalised medicine. It implements a multi-agent design with a temporal layer: versioned patient records, rule-based change detection, selective re-planning, claim-level provenance, adversarial validation, calibrated abstention and mandatory clinician sign-off before any suggestion reaches the patient.

The system uses rules encoded from peer-reviewed clinical guidelines (ADA 2024, KDIGO 2022/2024, FDA drug labelling, AGS Beers Criteria 2023, JACI 2022) to generate drug safety alerts, differential diagnoses and personalised treatment suggestions.

Built by Shivam Tomar. The website is the patient-facing and clinician-facing layer over the clinical AI engine.

Important limitations — please read

CareTrace is not a certified medical device and does not provide medical advice. It is a Clinical Decision Support tool designed to assist, not replace, a qualified clinician. In an emergency, call 112 or your local emergency number immediately. Never change your medication based solely on what you see here — all outputs require clinician review and approval.

  • The knowledge library covers the most common drug classes and clinical guidelines for diabetes, kidney disease, hypertension and anticoagulation. Medicines and conditions outside this scope are flagged as "not assessed".
  • Clinical decisions come from encoded guideline rules and evidence retrieval — not from a language model "guessing". The AI agents reason step-by-step from your data.
  • An optional Large Language Model (LLM) is used only for plain-language narration and complex escalation — it does not make clinical decisions and never receives your personal identifiers.
  • The evidence linker is lexical and can be affected by unusual phrasing. Every linked claim shows its source so clinicians can verify.

The complete list of known limitations is in docs/limitations.md, and the Research page shows evaluation results with their caveats.

Safety commitments built into the code

  • No output is presented to the patient as final until a named clinician has approved, modified or rejected it.
  • Every clinical claim carries a citation to a guideline or FDA label, or is withheld and flagged.
  • The system says when it is uncertain and defers instead of guessing — abstention is shown, never hidden.
  • All reviews and system events are written to a hash-chained audit log so tampering is detectable.
  • Your personal identifiers (name, date of birth) are never included in queries sent to any AI model.

Your data and privacy

Your health data is stored locally on the server running CareTrace. Lab values, medications and allergies you enter are associated only with your account. Free-text notes are screened to prevent accidental submission of contact details (email, phone, national ID). Please do not include other people's identifiable information in any field.
