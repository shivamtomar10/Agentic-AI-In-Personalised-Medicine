Accessibility

This site should work for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or magnification.

What we have done

  • Body text is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for readers with low vision.
  • Every page works with a keyboard, has a visible focus outline, and starts with a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Form fields have labels, and error messages are announced to screen readers and name the field.
  • Colour is never the only signal: statuses and changes also use words.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast targets in light and dark mode.
  • The layout adapts to small screens and to text enlarged to 200%. Animations are switched off if your system asks for reduced motion.

What we know is not perfect

  • The trend charts are drawn as images with a text description. A data table version is not yet offered.
  • The site has been tested by its author with a keyboard and automated checks, not by an external accessibility audit.

Report a barrier

If something is hard to use, tell us through the contact form and choose "Accessibility". Say what you were trying to do and which assistive technology you use, if any.
